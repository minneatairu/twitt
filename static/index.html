How your research data is processed

We send your query with lang:en -is:retweet to exclude reposts, UTC month boundaries, and X’s sort_order=relevancy. We preserve X’s order across pages, exclude posts not labeled English, retain the first occurrence of each post ID, and stop at your selected per-month limit. Each collection contains one selected month.

No custom scores, engagement thresholds, or engagement-based sorting are applied. Each metric is copied individually from X at collection time. raw_json contains the returned post, author, and attached media objects. CSV escapes formula-like text; Google Sheets stores literal values. Deleted or inaccessible posts are unavailable.

Choose a selected-posts CSV, then import it to show the reply search controls.

All English replies anywhere in each selected conversation, with no custom ranking. Conversation IDs match replies; direct-parent metadata is optional. Exports contain one row per unique reply ID. Every reply has a related selected tweet beside it. When its direct parent is not in the upload, we repeat the selected root tweet, or the first uploaded tweet from the same conversation. conversation_selected_post_ids lists the selected posts sharing the conversation without claiming they are the reply’s parent. Conversation IDs are combined into search batches up to X’s query-length limit; Reply totals in your uploaded CSV count direct replies and may be lower than whole-conversation results. The active job keeps its imported date window and page size. Editing these inputs affects only your next CSV import. Importing makes no API calls.
